# Returnables Website

The **Returnables Website** ([returnables.feds201.com](https://returnables.feds201.com/)) is used for our biggest fundraiser of the year: the can drive.

## Purpose

We promote QR codes with this website's link. People can enter their address and the time they want the pickup to occur.

This helps us manage our time and our fundraiser more effectively.

## Technical Details

-   **Code**: [github.com/feds201/Returnable-App](https://github.com/feds201/Returnable-App/blob/main/)
-   **Files**: `index.html` (HTML) and `script.js` (JavaScript).

## Accessing the Website

The website is live at [https://returnables.feds201.com/](https://returnables.feds201.com/).
