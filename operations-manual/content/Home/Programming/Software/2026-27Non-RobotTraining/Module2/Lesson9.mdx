# How the team builds components

Open `lib/components/` and look at `Button.dart`, `CheckBox.dart`, and `CounterShelf.dart`. Our components are **builder functions** that start with `build`: `buildButton(...)`, `buildCheckBox(...)`. Each one takes a few arguments and **returns a Widget**.

Look closely at `buildCheckBox` in `CheckBox.dart`. Can you tell what each of its arguments is for? Notice how it **tells the page** when it's tapped (through `onChanged`), but **doesn't remember anything itself**. The page keeps the state.
